137: The hypothesis
138: 
139: A useful agent system does not necessarily need one enormous autonomous context.
140: 
141: Instead, it can maintain durable external state describing:
142: 
143: - what we are trying to achieve;
144: - what we currently believe;
145: - what has been discovered;
146: - what remains uncertain;
147: - what work is available;
148: - what depends on what;
149: - what has already happened;
150: - where a human is required.
151: 
152: Agents can then operate on bounded pieces of that state.
153: 
154: This potentially allows different agents, models, tools and humans to collaborate without requiring a single continuous conversation.
155: 
156: The persistent graph is the continuity.
